Doctors as the
Guardrail

TerraComrad pairs AI mammogram analysis with the radiologist's judgment. The system scans each image at scale — segmenting masses, measuring shape and margin features, and scoring boundary characteristics with consistent precision. But precision is not a diagnosis. Here every algorithmic finding is evidence for the clinician to weigh, never a verdict to accept. Doctors draw their own regions of interest, interrogate the area-of-interest profile, and confirm or override what the model proposes — bringing patient history, clinical context, and expertise no algorithm can replicate. That is the guardrail model: AI narrows the search and structures the evidence; the physician decides what matters.
